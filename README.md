# Notes on Causal Inference

These are my personal study notes on causal inference, originally handwritten in a mixture of Chinese and English. They include lecture material, derivations, intuition, questions, and unfinished ideas.

An English translation of all four scanned notebooks is available below. The translation follows the handwritten notes and integrates facing-page supplements and arrow-linked annotations into their logical reading order. Uncertain handwriting and apparent source notation issues are explicitly marked as translator's notes.
